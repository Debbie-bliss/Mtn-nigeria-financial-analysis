# MTN Nigeria Financial Performance Analysis (2022–2024)

## Project Overview
This project analyses the financial performance of MTN Nigeria Communications Plc from 2022 to 2024 using published financial statements and Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to evaluate revenue growth, operating performance, profitability, and the impact of finance costs and foreign exchange losses on the company's financial results.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial statement analysis
- Financial ratio analysis
- Data visualization and dashboard reporting

## Key Areas of Analysis
- Revenue trends and year-on-year growth
- Profit/(loss) trends
- Operating profit margin
- Finance costs as a percentage of revenue
- Foreign exchange losses as a percentage of revenue
- Net profit margin
- Tax benefits in loss-making years

## Key Findings
- Revenue increased from ₦2,012,272 million in 2022 to ₦3,358,461 million in 2024.
- The company reported a profit of ₦358,877 million in 2022, followed by losses in 2023 and 2024.
- Operating profit remained positive throughout the period, while the company reported overall losses in 2023 and 2024.
- The analysis examines how finance costs and foreign exchange losses affected profitability.
